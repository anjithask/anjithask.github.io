My Story

Where purpose meets technology

I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), Adelaide University — where I've spent the past five years building AI that does more than optimise metrics; it protects ecosystems and improves lives.

My journey began at the intersection of software engineering and research, where I discovered that the most meaningful technical problems are human ones. Whether it's helping conservation scientists identify endangered species from camera-trap images, or making healthcare workflows more accessible through speech AI — I am drawn to the places where machine learning can quietly, meaningfully shift outcomes.

I believe the measure of good AI is not its accuracy score, but the world it helps create. That belief drives everything I build.

IRP2API — Automated Incident Response Mapping

Published at SANER 2023. A novel unsupervised transfer learning framework that automatically maps Incident Response Plans to security tool APIs, eliminating manual effort for SOC teams. Achieved 91.1% Top-15 Accuracy — a 41.6% improvement over the best non-transfer learning baseline — and processes each IRP task in just 0.8 seconds for real-world, time-critical deployment.

Green AI for Security Tool API Recommendation

Published at ENASE 2023. Challenged expensive Deep Learning approaches by proving ML models can match or exceed DL accuracy at a fraction of the cost. Logistic Regression with word & character features achieved 0.38% better accuracy than state-of-the-art DL, while reducing CPU core hours by 95.91%, model size by 97.65%, and processing time by 291.50% — making it viable for resource-constrained industrial SOC environments.
